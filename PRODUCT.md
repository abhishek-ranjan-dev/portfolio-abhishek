## Platform

web

## Users

Primary buyers this surface must convince:

1. **Startup founders / small SaaS teams** who need a complete product — marketing site and/or web app, increasingly with modern AI features (smart search, chatbots) — designed, built, and launched, but do not have (or do not want to assemble) an in-house engineering team.
2. **Small business & local brand owners** who need a polished, fast, trustworthy website and one dependable person to build and run it.

Both are evaluating whether a single independent engineer can credibly replace a whole tech team. They arrive skeptical, want proof of shipped work, honest timelines, and a direct line to the person doing the work.

## Product Purpose

A freelance portfolio / sales surface for Abhishek Ranjan. Its job is to win contract engagements by making the case that one senior engineer can design, build, and launch complete websites and web apps end-to-end. Success = qualified inbound briefs (email-first) from founders and small-business owners.

## Positioning

"One engineer. Whole tech team." A single senior engineer who personally does design, full-stack build, and launch — including modern AI features — with straight talk, honest timelines, and one accountable person the client can actually reach. The differentiator is *end-to-end ownership by one credible person*, positioned against both agencies (slow, layered, expensive) and gig-marketplace developers (cheap, unaccountable).

## Product Principles

1. **Prove, don't claim.** Credibility comes from real shipped work and real employers, shown — not adjectives.
2. **One accountable human.** Everything should reinforce that a real, reachable person does the work end-to-end.
3. **Straight talk.** Plain language, honest scope and timelines; no hype, no fake urgency, no invented social proof.
4. **Fast and sure.** The site itself is a work sample — performance, polish, and correctness are part of the pitch.
